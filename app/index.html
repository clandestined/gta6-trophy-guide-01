    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough (PowerPyx). Play the story first, protect the one missable trophy as you go, then clean up contacts, activities, collectibles and crime in that order, and finish with 100% completion. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 and about 200 hours.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy in the whole list is flagged as possibly missable by the guides. Everything else can be earned after the story in free roam.

      Do it as you go: meditate at every spot

      PowerPyx and GTABase both flag Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida) as the only possibly missable trophy, because one meditation spot can become unavailable later in the story. Meditate at every spot the moment you find it and never leave one for after the story.

        Story trophies, contact finales, activities, collectibles and crime trophies are all earnable after the final mission, so no backup save is needed for them. Cheats disable trophies and saving (GTABase / TechWiser), so keep cheats off for the whole run.
